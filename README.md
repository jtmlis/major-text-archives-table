# major-text-archives-table
A table of the major text archives of the world. 

All entries are in main.csv


What is a major text archive?

A major text archive is defined as having at least 10 million titles in its collection as per [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_the_world%27s_largest_libraries). Our list is more expansive than that of Wikipedia due to original research being allowed.

This list seeks to list every national archive/library, large academic archives, large non-profit archives, and other large institutions.
