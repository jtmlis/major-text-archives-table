# major-text-archives-table
A table of the major text archives of the world. 

This list seeks to list every national archive/library, large academic archives, large non-profit archives, and other large institutions.

## What is a major text archive?

A major text archive is defined as having at least 10 million books in its collection as per [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_the_world%27s_largest_libraries). Our list is more expansive than that of Wikipedia due to original research being allowed.

We group entries into 4 classifications
Huge = 10 million + books.
Large = 4 - 10 million books.
Medium = 1 - 4 million books.
Small = 0 to 1 million books.

This list focuses on obtaining metadata for large collections hence the focus on books. However, there is no standard way of measuring collection sizes and they can range from billions if you count webpages and versions of them to million if you only count books. 

If we were to allow other items, most libraries have hundreds of millions of articles and entries making the process a lot more complex.
